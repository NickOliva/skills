# Worked Answer Key

## Page 1
1. $3/8$: 3 of 8 equal parts are shaded. 2. Numerator 5 counts parts; denominator 7 names equal parts in the whole. 3. Any bar split into 6 equal parts with 4 shaded; $4/6$.

## Page 2
1. $3/5$ of 20 = $20\div5\times3=12$. 2. Blue $7/12$; not blue $5/12$. 3. $24\div4=6$ students.

## Page 3
1. $1/9$. 2. Eight $1/8$ pieces make 1 whole. 3. $1/3>1/6$ because thirds are larger equal parts.

## Page 4
1. $7/4=1\frac34$ (4 goes into 7 once, remainder 3). 2. $2\frac35=(2\times5+3)/5=13/5$. 3. One whole plus 1 of 2 parts; $1\frac12=3/2$.

## Page 5
1. Six equal intervals; fourth tick is $4/6$. 2. $3/8$. 3. $5/4=1\frac14$; mark one whole and one fourth.

## Page 6
1. Multiply by 4: $2/3=8/12$. 2. Multiply by 3: $5/8=15/24$. 3. Examples: $6/14$ and $9/21$.

## Page 7
1. Divide by 6: $12/18=2/3$. 2. Divide by 5: $20/25=4/5$. 3. Yes: $18\div6=3$ and $24\div6=4$, so $18/24=3/4$.

## Page 8
1. Four fourths of each half make four eighths, so both cover one-half. 2. $3/6=1/2$; also $6/12$. 3. True: $2/5\times2=4/10$.

## Page 9
1. Multiply by 5: $3/4=15/20$. 2. Multiply by 3: $7/9=21/27$. 3. Multiply by 3: $4/11=12/33$.

## Page 10
1. Divide by 2: $6/10=3/5$. 2. Divide by 4: $8/12=2/3$. 3. Divide by 5: $15/35=3/7$.

## Page 11
1. Divide by 4: $16/20=4/5$. 2. Divide by 7: $21/28=3/4$. 3. Yes; 9 and 13 have no common factor greater than 1.

## Page 12
1. Yes: $2/6$ divides by 2 to give $1/3$. 2. Multiply by 2: $3/4=6/8$ cup. 3. Divide by 2: $6/10=3/5$ mile.

## Page 13
1. $2/3=8/12$, so $=$. 2. $5/6=15/18$, so $=$. 3. $3/5=9/15$, so $=$.

## Page 14
1. Split one rectangle into thirds with 2 shaded; split another into ninths with 6 shaded. Both show $2/3=6/9$. 2. Divide into fourths with 3 shaded, or twelfths with 9 shaded. 3. Five groups with 2 shaded each gives $2/5$; fifteen parts with 6 shaded gives $6/15$.

## Page 15
1. Multiplying by 3 makes three copies of every original part: $a/b=(3a)/(3b)$, so the amount stays the same. 2. The denominator also changes; $4/8$ and $1/2$ are equal, not greater. 3. Multiply by 4: $5/6=20/24$.

## Page 16
1. $9/4=2\frac14$; converting back gives $(2\times4+1)/4=9/4$. 2. $1\frac23=(1\times3+2)/3=5/3$. 3. Yes: $2\frac12=(2\times2+1)/2=5/2$.

## Page 17
1. If 4 is $2/3$, one third is 2, so the whole is $2\times3=6$ parts. 2. $6\div3=2$ per fifth; $2\times5=10$ tiles. 3. $15\div5=3$ per eighth; $3\times8=24$ cm.

## Page 18
1. Invalid: adding changes the value. Multiply both terms by 2: $3/5=6/10$. 2. Not simplest: $6/8$ divides by 2 to $3/4$. 3. The denominator was multiplied by 2 without multiplying the numerator; correct form is $2/7=4/14$.

## Page 19
1. $24/32$ divides by 8 to $3/4$; denominator 20 requires multiply by 5: $15/20$. 2. $18/30$ divides by 6 to $3/5$; denominator 5 fraction is $3/5$. 3. $3/4<4/4<3/2$ (0.75, 1, 1.5).

## Page 20
1. $18/24$ divides by 6 to $3/4$; multiply by 2 for denominator 8: $6/8$. 2. Examples: $8/10$ (×2), $12/15$ (×3), $20/25$ (×5). 3. Multiply numerator and denominator by the same nonzero whole number; $7/10\times4=28/40$.

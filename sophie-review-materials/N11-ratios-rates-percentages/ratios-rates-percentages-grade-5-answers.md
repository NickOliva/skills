# Worked Answer Key

**Page 1:** 1. $6:4=3:2$. 2. There are 10 animals, so dogs to all is $7:10$.

**Page 2:** 1. Yes: $2\times4=8$ and $3\times4=12$. 2. Multiply both parts by 3: $15:6$; blank **6**.

**Page 3:** 1. $12\div3=4$ groups; $2\times4=8$ cups flour. 2. $5\times4=20$, so $4\times4=16$; blank **16**.

**Page 4:** 1. $9:6=3:2$. 2. Total $8+12=20$; fiction fraction $8/20=2/5$, ratio fiction to all $8:20=2:5$.

**Page 5:** 1. Compare with the same number of red: $6:4=42:28$ and $9:7=36:28$. Since $42>36$, the first group has more blue per red. 2. Yes: $3\times4=12$ and $4\times4=16$.

**Page 6:** 1. $8\div2=4$ groups; $3\times4=12$ walking laps. 2. $12\div4=3$ groups; $3\times3=9$ lemons.

**Page 7:** 1. $18\div6=\$3$ per notebook. 2. $240\div4=60$ miles per hour.

**Page 8:** 1. $5\times3=15$, $5\times7=35$, $5\times10=50$ pages. 2. $28\div4=\$7$ each; $7\times9=\$63$.

**Page 9:** 1. A: $6\div3=\$2$ each; B: $12\div5=\$2.40$ each. Store A is lower. 2. $18\div2=9$ minutes per mile; $9\times5=45$ minutes.

**Page 10:** 1. $12\div3=4$ liters per minute. 2. $45\div5=9$ pages per minute; $9\times8=72$ pages.

**Page 11:** 1. $35/100=0.35$. 2. $0.62=62/100=62$%.

**Page 12:** 1. $48/100=48$%. 2. $25\%=1/4$; $80\div4=20$.

**Page 13:** 1. $10\%=1/10$; $340\div10=34$. 2. $50\%=1/2$; $126\div2=63$.

**Page 14:** 1. $75\%=3/4$; $60\div4=15$, $15\times3=45$. 2. $20\%=2\times10\%$; $25+25=50$.

**Page 15:** 1. $30/120=1/4=25$%. 2. $40\%=4/10$; if $28$ is four tenths, one tenth is 7 and the whole is $70$.

**Page 16:** 1. $25\%=1/4$; $200\div4=50$ biographies. 2. $35\%$ of 80 is $28$; not tomatoes $80-28=52$.

**Page 17:** 1. $25\%$ of $40=\$10$; sale price $40-10=\$30$. 2. $10\%$ of $60=\$6$; sale price $60-6=\$54$.

**Page 18:** 1. $8\%$ of $50=\$4$; total $50+4=\$54$. 2. $20\%$ of $25=\$5$; total $25+5=\$30$.

**Page 19:** 1. Use a rate because each inch represents the same number of miles: $6\times4=24$ miles. 2. Use a percent because the question asks what part of all the games were wins, expressed per hundred: $18/24=3/4=75$%.

**Page 20:** 1. $240\div12=20$ boxes; $25\%=1/4$; $20\div4=5$ blue boxes. 2. $36\div3=12$ miles/hour; $12\times5=60$ miles; $60/100=60$%.

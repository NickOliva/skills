# Worked Answer Key

## Page 1
1. Diego read the most, with 7 books. He read $7-4=3$ more than Chloe.
2. $6+9+4+7=26$ books.

## Page 2
1. $24+27=51$ plants.
2. $27-15=12$ plants.

## Page 3
1. $36+18+12+9=75$ pounds.
2. $36-12=24$ pounds.

## Page 4
1. Kai: $18+24+21=63$ pages.
2. Mia: $25+19+26=70$; $70-63=7$ pages greater.

## Page 5
1. $8+6+5+3=22$ students.
2. Apples: $8/22=4/11$.

## Page 6
1. 4 inches appears three times, so 4 inches is most common.
2. $6-2=4$ inches.

## Page 7
1. $1+2+2+3+3+3+4+6=24$ stickers.
2. Values at least 3 are 3, 3, 3, 4, and 6: 5 cards.

## Page 8
1. Sum $=72+80+88+90+70=400$; $400/5=80$.
2. New sum $=400+82=482$; $482/6=80\frac{1}{3}$, or about 80.3.

## Page 9
1. Ordered: 68, 69, 70, 72, 72, 72, 75. The middle value is 72°.
2. Mode is 72° because it appears three times.

## Page 10
1. Range $=25-10=15$ minutes.
2. New maximum is 30; range $=30-10=20$. It increases by 5 minutes.

## Page 11
1. Thursday, with 50 visitors.
2. $32+45+38=115$ visitors.

## Page 12
1. $14-6=8$ more cats.
2. Dogs: $9/32=0.28125$, or 28.125% (about 28.1%).

## Page 13
1. Blue scored more in Games 1 and 4: 22>18 and 25>16.
2. Red: $18+24+20+16=78$. Blue: $22+19+20+25=86$.

## Page 14
1. Four stars × 4 students = 16 students.
2. Art and Drama: $(3+1)×4=16$ students.

## Page 15
1. Oranges: $8/40=1/5$.
2. Apples or bananas: $(15+10)/40=25/40=5/8$.

## Page 16
1. There are $5+3+2=10$ marbles; blue probability $=3/10$.
2. Red is more likely: 5 red marbles compared with 2 green marbles.

## Page 17
1. $125+178=303$ tickets.
2. $303×4=1{,}212$ dollars.

## Page 18
1. Growth: Week 1→2 is 3 cm, Week 2→3 is 4 cm, Week 3→4 is 1 cm. Most growth was between Weeks 2 and 3.
2. $14-6=8$ cm.

## Page 19
1. A bar graph compares separate categories or months clearly; a line graph is better for showing change over a continuous time sequence. Either choice can be defended if the reason matches the graph. For separate monthly totals, bar graph is the best choice.
2. $8+5+11+7=31$ rainy days.

## Page 20
1. Ordered votes: 5, 8, 12, 15. With four values, median is the mean of middle two: $(8+12)/2=10$ votes.
2. Total votes $=12+8+15+5=40$. Game day: $15/40=3/8=37.5\%$.

# Worked Answer Key

## Page 1

1. $8+3\times4=8+12=20$.
2. $24-12\div3=24-4=20$.
3. $7\times5+6=35+6=41$.

## Page 2

1. $(8+3)\times4=11\times4=44$.
2. $24-(12\div3)=24-4=20$.
3. $7\times(5+6)=7\times11=77$.

## Page 3

1. $36\div4\times3=9\times3=27$.
2. $5\times6\div2=30\div2=15$.
3. $48\div6\times2+1=8\times2+1=17$.

## Page 4

1. $32-7+5=25+5=30$.
2. $18+9-6=27-6=21$.
3. $50-14+8-6=36+8-6=38$.

## Page 5

1. $9+18\div3=9+6=15$.
2. $6\times7-20=42-20=22$.
3. $45\div5+4\times3=9+12=21$.

## Page 6

1. $3\times(12-5)+8=3\times7+8=29$.
2. $64\div(4+4)+7=64\div8+7=15$.
3. $40-2\times(6+3)=40-18=22$.

## Page 7

1. $4\times8+6=32+6=38$.
2. $72\div8-9=9-9=0$.
3. $(5+7)\times3=12\times3=36$.

## Page 8

1. $(6+4)\times5=50$.
2. $(18-6)\div3=4$.
3. $(2+8)\times3-1=30-1=29$.

## Page 9

1. $x=65-27=38$; $38+27=65$.
2. $n=300-148=152$; $148+152=300$.
3. $m=1{,}000-425=575$; $575+425=1{,}000$.

## Page 10

1. $y=46+19=65$; $65-19=46$.
2. $p=92-37=55$; $92-55=37$.
3. $q=425+275=700$; $700-275=425$.

## Page 11

1. $a=36\div4=9$.
2. $b=84\div7=12$.
3. $c=144\div12=12$.

## Page 12

1. $d=9\times5=45$.
2. $e=11\times8=88$.
3. $f=156\div12=13$.

## Page 13

1. $2x+5=19$; $2x=14$; $x=7$.
2. $3y-4=20$; $3y=24$; $y=8$.
3. $n\div4+6=15$; $n\div4=9$; $n=36$.

## Page 14

1. $(x+3)\times4=28$; $x+3=7$; $x=4$.
2. $5(p-2)=35$; $p-2=7$; $p=9$.
3. $q+6\times7=80$; $q+42=80$; $q=38$.

## Page 15

1. $x+38=91$; $x=53$.
2. $6p=54$; $p=9$ pencils per pack.
3. $x-24=57$; $x=81$.

## Page 16

1. $4\times36+18=144+18=162$ students.
2. $(60-14)\div2=46\div2=23$ dollars per jar.
3. $5\times24-17=120-17=103$ rolls.

## Page 17

1. $6+4\times5=26$ and $(6+4)\times5=50$; $26<50$.
2. $48\div6+2=10$ and $48\div(6+2)=6$; $10>6$.
3. $3\times(9-2)=21$ and $3\times9-2=25$; $21<25$.

## Page 18

## Page 19

1. $90-4\times(8+2)=90-4\times10=50$.

1. $5(x+2)=45$; $x+2=9$; $x=7$.

# Worked Answer Key

For a mean, add all values and divide by the number of values. For a median, order the values first. Range is greatest minus least.

## Page 1
1. Ordered 4, 6, 6, 8, 11; sum 35; mean 7; median 6; mode 6; range 11 − 4 = 7.
2. Ordered 3, 5, 7, 7, 8, 12; sum 42; mean 7; median (7 + 7) ÷ 2 = 7; mode 7; range 12 − 3 = 9.

## Page 2
1. (12 + 16 + 20 + 24) ÷ 4 = 72 ÷ 4 = **18**.
2. (7 + 9 + 10 + 12 + 17) ÷ 5 = 55 ÷ 5 = **11 cans**.

## Page 3

2. 96 ÷ 4 = **24 points**.

## Page 17

## Page 20

**p20-01.** Translate right: $(1,2),(5,2),(5,5),(1,5)\to(3,2),(7,2),(7,5),(3,5)$. Reflect across $y=4$: y-coordinate 2 becomes 6, and 5 becomes 3, keeping equal distances on opposite sides of the line. The final vertices, in order, are $(3,6),(7,6),(7,3),(3,3)$.

**p20-02.** Sample answer: both transformations preserve size and shape. A translation slides every point the same distance in the same direction; a reflection flips a figure across a mirror line. Example translation 2 units right: $(1,2)\to(3,2)$. Example reflection across $x=4$: $(1,2)\to(7,2)$, since both points are 3 units from that line. Other correct similarities, differences, and examples are acceptable.

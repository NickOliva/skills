## Page 1

**p01-01.** The first number is the x-coordinate (horizontal movement); the second is the y-coordinate (vertical movement). For $(4,2)$, move 4 right and 2 up.

**p01-02.** Correct plot: $A(2,5)$ and $B(5,1)$.

## Page 2

**p02-01.** $(3,6)$: 3 units right, then 6 units up.

**p02-02.** x-coordinate 7; y-coordinate 4; move 7 right and 4 up.

## Page 3

**p03-02.** $H(2,6)$ is highest. $I$ and $J$ share x-coordinate 4.

**p04-02.** $(6,5-3)=(6,2)$. Only y changes; moving down decreases y.

**p09-01.** It stays $(0,4)$ because a point on the y-axis is the same distance (zero) from the y-axis after reflection.

**p09-02.** The blank is $-3$: $(5,3)$ reflects to $(5,-3)$ across the x-axis.

## Page 10

**p10-01.** First quarter-turn clockwise: right. Second: down.

**p10-02.** The square’s center is $(2,2)$ and the square is symmetric, so a quarter-turn maps the same set of vertices to itself (the labels move positions).

## Page 11

**p11-01.** $G=(6,4)$. Two units right of $(4,4)$ rotated clockwise becomes two units below: $G'=(4,2)$.

**p11-02.** $H=(5,8)$. Three units above $(5,5)$ rotated counterclockwise becomes three units left: $H'=(2,5)$.

## Page 12

**p12-01.** $7-2=5$ units.

**p12-02.** $6-1=5$ units.

## Page 13

**p13-01.** Width $6-1=5$, height $4-1=3$; perimeter $2(5+3)=16$ units.

**p13-02.** Side length $5-2=3$; perimeter $4\times3=12$ units.

## Page 14

**p14-01.** Width $8-2=6$, height $4-1=3$; area $6\times3=18$ square units.

**p14-02.** Side length $7-3=4$; area $4\times4=16$ square units.

## Page 15

**p15-01.** Yes. Congruent figures have the same shape and size; both rectangles have corresponding sides 4 and 7.

**p15-02.** A translation moves every point the same distance and direction, so size and shape stay the same. The figures are congruent.

## Page 16

**p16-01.** A translation slides a figure; every point moves the same distance in the same direction.

**p16-02.** Reflection across the y-axis: $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,y)$, so $(4,2)\mapsto(-4,2)$.

## Page 17

**p17-01.** Translate: $(2,3)\to(4,3)$. Reflect across y-axis: $(4,3)\to(-4,3)$.

**p17-02.** Reflect: $(5,1)\to(5,-1)$. Translate: $(5,-1)\to(4,1)$.

## Page 18

**p18-01.** Same x-coordinate, so vertical distance is $5-1=4$ blocks.

**p18-02.** East increases x: $3+4=7$. South decreases y: $4-3=1$. Stop at $(7,1)$.

## Page 19

**p19-01.** Lower-left $(1,1)$; vertices are $(1,1),(4,1),(4,3),(1,3)$.

**p19-02.** Horizontal side length $4-1=3$; vertical side length $5-2=3$.

## Page 20

**p20-01.** Translate left: $(1,2),(5,2),(5,5),(1,5)\to(-1,2),(3,2),(3,5),(-1,5)$. Reflect x-axis: $(-1,-2),(3,-2),(3,-5),(-1,-5)$.

**p20-02.** Similarity: both transformations preserve size and shape. Difference: a translation slides every point; a reflection flips across a line. Example translation $(1,2)\to(3,2)$; y-axis reflection $(1,2)\to(-1,2)$.

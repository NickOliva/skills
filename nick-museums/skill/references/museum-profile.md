# Museum Profile and Registry

Use this runtime profile to rank museum opportunities for Nick. Verify all volatile details at the time of research; the registry is a baseline, not a frozen list.

## Travel and privacy

- Use the origin in `private.local.md` when present; otherwise use `Mamaroneck, NY`.
- Identify the origin publicly only as `Mamaroneck, NY`. Never reproduce or submit a private street address without explicit authorization for that transaction.
- The normal regional universe extends roughly to the road distance between Mamaroneck and Boston: about three hours in favorable conditions, with an outer band for exceptional or overnight-worthy opportunities.

## Art priorities

Highest interests:

- Impressionism and Post-Impressionism, especially Claude Monet, Paul Cézanne, and Gustave Caillebotte.
- J. M. W. Turner, especially *The Fighting Temeraire*, plus British Romantic, atmospheric, and pre-Impressionist painting.
- Major European painting and recognized masters, including Leonardo da Vinci and El Greco.
- Hudson River School painting, though below Impressionism in priority.
- Chinese painting and handscrolls, especially Northern Song landscapes and rare rotations or loans of canonical scrolls.
- Artistically or historically significant photography, including Richard Avedon, large-format work, landscape, and photojournalism.
- Modern artists only when their individual work is a demonstrated fit; do not infer preference from the label “modern.”

Top-priority work:

- Wen Zhengming, *Deep Snow in Mountain Passes* (關山積雪圖; also translated *Heavy Snow in the Mountain Passes*), 1532, Ming dynasty, handscroll, National Palace Museum, Taipei. Any verified display, loan, or focused exhibition is potentially fly-worthy. Never infer that it is on view from collection ownership.

Negative preferences and filters:

- Strongly disfavor abstract, conceptual, avant-garde, and deliberately provocative work whose primary value is interpretive rather than visual or technical.
- Exclude AI-generated photography or imagery.
- Religious subject matter lowers priority but does not disqualify otherwise exceptional work by artists such as El Greco or Leonardo.
- Exclude commercial galleries by default. Museum-quality academic spaces and major nonprofit photography institutions may qualify.
- Judge the specific exhibition and works on view rather than accepting or rejecting an institution by category.
- If no temporary exhibition clears the threshold, prefer a strong permanent-collection visit instead of lowering the standard.

## Ranking rules

Rank by preference fit, artistic importance or rarity, verified availability, travel burden, usable gallery time, cost when material, and confidence. Recommend air travel only for an exceptional match. Preserve newly stated preferences without turning a one-time choice into a permanent rule.

## Worldwide watchlist

Search worldwide for major retrospectives, reunions, rare rotations, and exceptional loans involving Turner, Monet, Cézanne, Caillebotte, Leonardo, El Greco, Wen Zhengming’s *Deep Snow in Mountain Passes*, Northern Song and other canonical Chinese handscrolls, Richard Avedon, and major landscape or photojournalism exhibitions. Surface qualifying opportunities for awareness even without current travel intent, but distinguish awareness from an actual travel recommendation.
